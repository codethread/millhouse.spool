# Millstrand-workflows cookbook

Recipes here combine multiple workflow families; parameter shapes and focused starts live in the generated API.

## Publish and adopt a producer export

**Situation.** A macro-owning spool has changed its public forms and a consumer must adopt the export without taking ownership of producer mappings.

**Composition.** Run `publish-spool-kondo` in the producer checkout, review its export and tests, then run `bootstrap-kondo` in the consumer. Select `greenfield` only for a missing local boundary; select `brownfield` to inventory and merge an existing one. The consumer bootstrap parses owned roots once, removes consumer-owned producer exports before its one resolved-classpath copy, retains true dependency exports, validates provenance and cache hygiene, and discovers the consumer's own quality checks.

**Why this shape.** Publication establishes one producer source of truth before adoption. Keeping the two workflows in separate worktrees makes ownership, self-import checks, and pending local work explicit. A local-self consumer excludes its own producer exports before copying; an ordinary remote consumer keeps the pinned producer exports as dependencies. Ambiguous ownership stops the import before it can write config.

## Bump a pinned family and adopt its exports

**Situation.** A consumer pins Millstrand or another spool family and needs the newest approved default-branch commit.

**Composition.** Give `bump-spool` family names plus the exact consumer worktree and workspace. It emits one `spool bump <family> --latest sha` request per family, accepts an already-current coordinate, calls `bootstrap-kondo`, and refreshes the selected runtime. Choose `app`, `spool`, or `clojure-app` when prompted. If producer alignment changes an active coordinate, its proof runs refresh explicitly. A fully applied result continues normally. The only pending result accepted for tooling is top-level `:status :partial` with nonempty module outcomes. Every top-level `:modules` map key must equal its outcome `:module/key`; reject any map-key identity mismatch. Each outcome is unchanged, a direct refused hard-conflict with exact root evidence, or a finite acyclic failed/skipped missing-dependency chain whose dependency matches the nested module key at every hop and terminates in that direct refusal. Every direct refused hard-conflict terminal, whether top-level or reached through a missing-dependency chain, must carry `:root-lib` equal to exactly one `:lib` in the declared nonempty changed-root set; reject any unrelated or mismatched terminal root-lib. Applied, malformed, cyclic, unrelated, or mixed outcomes fail loudly. Every direct conflict has exactly `:changed-roots` and `:namespace-residuals`, and the pending-generation `:diff` equals that complete classification. Changed roots and residual bindings/providers reconcile matching libs, roots, namespaces, and distinct nonempty paths. Record current and prepared generations, use prepared roots for Kondo, LSP, and tools.deps, and leave adoption unfinished. With no coordinate change, run the same full `(runtime/refresh! (current/runtime))` and record it with read-only `(runtime/status (current/runtime))` and `spool status`; accept only top-level refresh `:status :unchanged`, every module in `:modules` with `:status :unchanged`, runtime `:pending-generation nil`, and synced roots under spool status family/root `:roots` projections. Applied, partial, refused, error, other module statuses, non-nil pending generation, and malformed or contradictory results fail loudly. The chosen continuation asks the agent to align tools.deps, LSP, lint, tests, and Weaver behavior with the effective spool world before handover or an authorized cutover.

**Why this shape.** Family-only input prevents callers from inventing versions or SHAs. Reusing bootstrap keeps Kondo ownership and provenance identical after a bump. The repository-style steps stay manual because each consumer has different aliases, commands, and source ownership; they do not add a universal gate.

The no-coordinate proof uses the same exact grammar: every `:modules` map key equals its outcome `:module/key`; every unchanged module has `:status :unchanged` and no `:error`, `:reason`, refusal, `:root/outcome`, `:dependency`, or `:dependency/outcome`; and spool status covers exactly the intended family/root set. Each intended root is `:status :synced` with a `:sync` map and a nonempty `:root` (`:sync.root`) matching active-root evidence. Failed, conflicted, source-reload, partial, missing, extra, mismatched, or invalid-root evidence fails loudly.

## Choose a local or pinned Millstrand update

**Situation.** A consumer's Millstrand coordinate may be a sibling checkout during development or a Git/SHA pin in a shared checkout.

**Composition.** Start `bump-millstrand`, inspect the exact `deps-file`, and route explicitly. The local route preserves the checkout, asks for a move-forward decision, then calls `bootstrap-kondo`; the pinned route delegates to `bump-spool`. Both routes refresh the selected runtime and ask for the same `app`, `spool`, or `clojure-app` tooling choice. When a generation is pending, record what works against the current generation and leave the chosen Weaver check unfinished. Hand over unless the direct user has authorized cutover; after cutover, repeat that check against the adopted generation.

**Why this shape.** Local development stays local, while pinned consumers use the remote default-branch SHA path. Both get the same tooling contract without pretending that `deps.edn` replaces spool approval. The explicit boundary prevents an agent or nested workflow from stopping or restarting a runtime.

## Configure tooling without changing a coordinate

**Situation.** A consumer already has the intended spool coordinates but its Clojure tools do not see the same source, config, or tests as its Weaver.

The pending evidence contract is exact: accept only top-level `:status :partial` with nonempty module outcomes. Every top-level `:modules` map key must equal its outcome `:module/key`; reject any map-key identity mismatch. Each outcome is unchanged, a direct refused hard-conflict with exact root evidence, or a finite acyclic failed/skipped missing-dependency chain whose dependency matches the nested module key at every hop and terminates in that direct refusal. Every direct refused hard-conflict terminal, whether top-level or reached through a missing-dependency chain, must carry `:root-lib` equal to exactly one `:lib` in the declared nonempty changed-root set; reject any unrelated or mismatched terminal root-lib. Applied, malformed, cyclic, unrelated, and mixed outcomes fail loudly. Every direct conflict has exactly `:changed-roots` and `:namespace-residuals`, and the pending-generation `:diff` equals that complete classification. Residual bindings and providers reconcile matching libs, namespaces, old/new roots, and distinct nonempty provider paths. With no coordinate change, run the full `(runtime/refresh! (current/runtime))` and record it with read-only `(runtime/status (current/runtime))` and `spool status`; accept only top-level refresh `:status :unchanged`, every module in `:modules` with `:status :unchanged`, runtime `:pending-generation nil`, and synced roots under spool status family/root `:roots` projections. Applied, partial, refused, error, other module statuses, non-nil pending generation, and malformed or contradictory results fail loudly.

**Composition.** Call `configure-consumer-tooling` with the exact consumer worktree, its derived `worktree/.millstrand` workspace, and the invocation producer coordinate. During inspection, first run exact consumer-world status; on `mill/no-selected-weaver`, start only that disposable consumer Weaver, record its PID and identities, and rerun status before repository classification. Never pass the disposable workflow-host workspace to this child. Use a pinned remote `millhouse/spools` family with its exact URL and full SHA for ordinary consumers, or the exact local self root and `local/root` when Millhouse is the consumer. Inspect spool status and repository conventions, then choose one style. The routed bootstrap confirms the same exact target idempotently and does not acquire or restart a Weaver. Before bootstrap, compare the supplied coordinate with `spools.edn` and every relevant Millhouse root in `deps.edn`. Manually align only the mismatched coordinates, preserve each `:deps/root`, and record the before and after values. When alignment changes an active coordinate, run `(runtime/refresh! (current/runtime))` and record its result. Accept a fully applied result or the exact nested partial outcome grammar only: unchanged, direct refused hard-conflict, or finite acyclic missing-dependency wrappers ending in that direct refusal. Its conflict has only `:changed-roots` and `:namespace-residuals`, and the pending `:diff` equals it; every residual must reconcile its binding/`:providers` entries to one recorded old/new root pair. Extra keys, unrelated paths or providers, applied/mixed outcomes, and ambiguous mappings fail loudly. Record current and prepared generations, use prepared roots for tooling, and leave Weaver adoption unfinished. Stop loudly on any other partial or error result. Stop when metadata is missing or cannot be compared. Do not infer the running workflow SHA or automate the edits. Then work through the tools.deps, LSP, lint, test, and Weaver steps, recording the real commands and results.

**Why this shape.** Clojure tools consume a static tools.deps view, while the Weaver consumes approved spool roots. The workflow makes the two views agree where needed without merging their authority or imposing one repository layout.

The no-coordinate proof is exact here too: derive the intended family/root set from the selected activation and relevant producer metadata, require exact `:families`/`:roots` coverage, then require every root outcome to be `:status :synced` with a nonempty `:sync.root` matching its recorded active-root evidence. Missing, extra, mismatched, non-synced, or invalid-root evidence fails loudly.
